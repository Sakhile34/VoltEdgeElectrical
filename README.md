## Project Overview
VoltEdge Electrical is a website built for a fictional electrical services company based in Mbombela. The site provides information about the company, its services, and contact details. It allows users to browse services, submit enquiries, and contact the business through interactive forms. The website was built using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## Website Goals and Objectives
- Provide a professional online presence for VoltEdge Electrical
- Clearly showcase the company's services
- Allow potential customers to easily make enquiries
- Provide contact information and location details for multiple branches
- Ensure the website is responsive and accessible on all devices
- Improve usability and search visibility through JavaScript and SEO

## Website Pages
- Home Page (index.html) – Introduction and overview of services
- About Us (about.html) – Company history, mission, vision, team
- Services (services.html) – Detailed list of services offered, with search
- Contact Us (contact.html) – Contact details, locations, and contact form
- Enquiry Form (enquiry.html) – Enquiry form for potential customers
